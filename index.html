What's Inside

Packed With Features

Everything you need for a powerful WhatsApp bot, all in one package.

AI Integration

Powered by Google Gemini AI — ask anything, generate images, translate and more.

Media Downloader

Download from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest and dozens more.

Group Management

Anti-link, anti-delete, welcome/goodbye, tag all, promote, demote and more.

Guardian System

Anti-delete, anti-edit, anti-call, anti-bot and full message protection built-in.
